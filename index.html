OFFICIAL LEAGUE GUIDE · 2026–27

📖 League Rules

The roster, scoring, waivers, schedule and league traditions in one place. The handbook is the reference for any commissioner interpretation.

🏐 Rosters & lineup lock

11 assets per manager: 9 starters (4 OH, 2 MB, 1 setter, 1 libero and 1 OUA university team), plus 2 bench athletes of any player position. Outside, left-side, right-side and opposite hitters qualify for OH.

Players and university teams can only have one fantasy owner. Bench points are visible but do not count toward weekly matchup scores unless moved to an eligible starting position before lineup lock. Lineups and transactions lock one hour before the first OUA game of that fantasy week. Only OUA regular-season matches earn points.

Libero digs and middle-blocker blocks use their enhanced point value instead of the standard value, not in addition to it.

University team scoring: set won +1; match won +1; sweep +1; reverse sweep +5.

🎟️ Draft format

Six managers, eleven snake-draft rounds (66 picks total). First-round order: Amit → Alex → Robbie → Owen → Steve → Josh. Even-numbered rounds reverse the order. Commissioner records draft picks in the shared Draft Room.

📅 Regular season, standings & playoffs

Nine regular fantasy weeks and three playoff weeks. Week 6 is Battle of the Seeds; Week 7 is David vs Goliath; Week 8 is Ex’s & OHs; Week 9 is Rivalry Week.

Standings use head-to-head wins and losses, then cumulative fantasy points. A tied matchup is broken by higher season cumulative FP (including that week), then higher entering-week rank or seed.

All six GMs qualify: Week 10 quarterfinals (seeds 1–2 get byes; seed 3 chooses between seeds 5–6); Week 11 semifinals (seed 1 selects a quarterfinal winner); Week 12 championship, bronze and fifth-place matchups.

💰 Waivers & free agency

Every GM starts with 100 Waiver Bucks (WB). Blind waiver claims close Tuesday at 11:59 PM Toronto time. The commissioner processes bids Wednesday: highest bid wins, losing bids cost 0 WB. Ties go to the lower-ranked GM; Week 1 uses reverse draft order as the tiebreaker.

Once waivers process, free agency is first come, first served, at 0 WB until weekly roster lock. Unused WB carry forward, and awards may raise balances above 100. Legal roster composition and a nonnegative wallet must be maintained.

🏅 Extra WB, penalties & league shenanigans
  • Chirp of the Week: +5 WB for the best roast or meme, voted by the league.
  • Get Licht Tax: −5 WB for a full week of group-chat silence.
  • Bounty of the Week: +5 WB for beating the first-ranked GM entering the week (regular season).
  • Comeback King: +5 WB for the biggest increase in weekly FP over the previous week.
  • Weekly Press Conference: Biggest matchup loser delivers a post-game press conference.
  • Compliment Rule: Lowest scorer sincerely compliments the highest scorer.

Only the first four adjust WB. Commissioner awards and penalties will be recorded in the league's transaction ledger.

Unofficial, non-commercial league. Rules are based on the league handbook; changes require a commissioner decision.
